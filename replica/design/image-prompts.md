# Recursos generados con ImageGen

Modo: herramienta integrada `image_gen`, fondo transparente; sin CLI ni API key.
Los PNG finales se copiaron a `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`.

## Logo — `neko_logo.png`

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Android launcher icon and in-app header logo for Neko Player.
Primary request: Create a polished, adorable white kitten HEAD wearing purple over-ear headphones, visually consistent with the kitten logo in the supplied Neko Player interface reference. Reference image role: style, palette and character reference only. Clean symmetrical frontal kitten face, large dark violet eyes with white highlights, tiny pink nose, happy little w mouth, soft rosy cheeks, triangular white ears with pink inner ears, deep purple outlines, glossy lavender/violet headphone cushions and curved headband. Rounded vector-like illustration with gentle gradients, legible at 48 px. Head occupies 85% of square with comfortable transparent padding; ears and headphones completely visible. Transparent background, no enclosing tile or circle. No words, no letters, no watermark, no extra objects. Output a single centered logo, not a sheet of variants.

## Mascota — `neko_mascot.png`

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: Transparent mascot illustration for the hero of Neko Player, an Android music app.
Primary request: A single full-body cute white chibi kitten dancing joyfully while wearing glossy purple over-ear headphones. Match the supplied lavender Neko Player UI's soft illustrated style: smooth cream-white fur, deep plum rounded outlines, huge round head, tiny rounded pear body, pink triangular inner ears, crescent closed smiling eyes, blushing pink cheeks, tiny pink nose, delighted open mouth with pink tongue. Left paw lifted in a wave with pink paw pads visible, right paw outstretched, one foot raised, curled fluffy tail to the right. Headphones wrap over the top between the ears, with rich violet pads. Flat vector-like kawaii illustration, refined gentle lilac shading and warm white highlights, no hair tufts, clean simple silhouette. Portrait composition, entire figure visible with 5% transparent padding, centered, no cropping. Transparent background. No words, no letters, no musical notes, no shadows outside the character, no border, no watermark. The surrounding music notes and background will be animated separately in the app.

### Edición final de la mascota

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied dancing headphone kitten. Change ONLY the background: remove ALL purple haze, glow, halo, dark backdrop and shadows outside the kitten. Deliver a clean isolated character on actual fully transparent alpha background. Preserve the kitten's pose, body proportions, cream white fur, facial expression, plum outlines, pink pads and glossy purple headphones exactly. All pixels outside the crisp character silhouette must be fully transparent, including gaps around ears, arm and tail. Do not add any glow or shadow. No new objects or text.
